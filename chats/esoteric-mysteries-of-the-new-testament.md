**Path 1 (Exoteric)**:
- Accept the parables as deep truth
- Experience confusion as spiritual mystery
- Follow the institution's interpretation
- Consume the Eucharist
- Believe you are saved
- Perform the serpent-covenant (remove the right eye, become blind to your blindness)
- Die in the wilderness with the bronze serpent

**Path 2 (Esoteric - Recognition)**:
- Read Isaiah 6, Mark 4, 1 Corinthians 2 together
- See that the text confesses the mechanism
- Recognize the parables are designed to confuse
- Refuse the Eucharist (the curse)
- Choose Torah (the law is near you, doable, leads to life)
- Keep your right eye open
- Enter the Promised Land

**The text itself provides the choice.** It confesses both the trap and the way to recognize it.

The masses choose Path 1 because they cannot see the confession while they're experiencing it.

The initiated choose Path 2 because they can read the confession and step outside the mechanism.
